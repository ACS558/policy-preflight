POLICY CHANGE PREFLIGHT

TRUSTED GUIDANCE

Knowledge version: 1.0

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Document type: Fictional demonstration knowledge

Scope: Internal policy changes only.

This knowledge source is fictional demonstration material created specifically for the Agent-a-thon prototype. It does not represent the policies of a real organization.

1. CHANGE POLICY

Every internal policy change must include:

  • A clear summary of the proposed change
  • The rationale for the change
  • A list of impacted teams
  • A risk rating
  • A proposed rollout date
  • A rollback plan

Approval chain: Policy Owner → Department Head → Governance Council

Minimum review period: Policy changes require a minimum review period of 5 business days before approval.

2. SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE

Policies affecting customer data must align with the Data Handling Standard.

Any policy change that reduces the strength of an existing control requires Security review.

If compliance language is unclear, the change must be escalated to Governance for clarification.

3. BRAND AND COMMUNICATIONS

Customer-facing policy text must follow the Plain Language Style Guide.

Major policy changes require a communication plan containing:

  • Target audience
  • Communication channel
  • Communication timing
  • Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

4. PREFLIGHT CHECKLIST

Before a policy change proceeds, review the following areas.

Dependencies

  • Related policies
  • Related processes
  • Related tools

Impacted Teams

  • Teams affected by the change
  • Relevant owners
  • Required reviewers

Compliance

  • Customer-data impact
  • Privacy impact
  • Regulatory impact

Risk

  • Low, medium, or high rating
  • One-line justification

Rollout

  • Proposed date
  • Rollout steps
  • Responsible owner

Rollback

  • Trigger conditions
  • Rollback steps

Communication

  • Communication plan for major policy changes
  • Audience
  • Channel
  • Timing
  • FAQ

5. ESCALATION RULES

Missing rollback plan: Escalate to the Policy Owner.

Security or compliance ambiguity: Escalate to Security or Governance.

Conflicting guidance: Pause the change and escalate to the Governance Council.

6A. RISK ASSESSMENT LIMITATION

The knowledge source requires every policy change to have a low, medium, or high risk rating with a one-line justification.

However, this knowledge source does not define objective criteria for assigning a specific risk rating.

Therefore, when sufficient information is not available, the assistant must identify relevant risk factors and state that the final risk rating requires human assessment.

The assistant must not invent a risk rating merely to complete the checklist.

6B. MAJOR POLICY CHANGE CLASSIFICATION LIMITATION

Section 3 requires a communication plan for major policy changes.

However, this knowledge source does not define objective criteria for determining whether a policy change is major or non-major.

Therefore, when the proposal does not clearly establish whether the change is a major policy change, the assistant must identify this as Needs verification and state that a human determination of major or non-major status is required before the communication requirement can be confirmed.

The assistant must not assume that a policy change is major or non-major merely to complete the checklist.

Important distinction:

"Major policy change" refers to the type or scale of the policy change.

"Major" as a finding severity refers only to the priority of an identified finding.

7. INFORMATION LIMITATIONS

This knowledge source does not contain:

  • Specific organizational structure beyond the stated approval chain
  • Actual employee or customer data
  • Actual contracts
  • Legal advice
  • Detailed regulatory requirements
  • Actual IT-system architecture
  • Actual business processes
  • Actual customer records

When information is not available, the assistant must identify it as Needs verification rather than assuming an answer.

The assistant must not guess, infer unsupported requirements, or present assumptions as facts.
